### 🔬 About Me

I work on making images and videos more useful when capture conditions are imperfect. As a PhD candidate in Computer Science at **NTNU**, I study deep learning for video enhancement in pre-hospital medical services, where the quality of a video can matter to clinical communication.

My experience spans optical depth-of-field expansion at COGNEX, hyperspectral imaging for cultural heritage, and object detection deployed on mobile devices. These projects shape my interest in connecting imaging hardware, camera pipelines, and machine learning.

### 🧭 Research Interests

My research focuses on **efficient and reliable machine learning for computational imaging**. I am interested in **inverse imaging**, particularly image/video restoration and enhancement, and **image/video quality assessment** to guide reconstruction under real-world distortions. My work combines data-efficient learning, including few-shot and contrastive methods, with uncertainty quantification and robustness under dataset shift.

### 🛠 What I'm Working On

- **All-in-one video reconstruction** for pre-hospital video communication during my visiting research at HTW Berlin.
- **Image/video distortion classification** using data-efficient learning and evaluation under dataset shift.
- **Video stability quality assessment**, including the STAViQ manuscript in preparation.
