## Research interests

My research focuses on **efficient and reliable machine learning for computational imaging**. I am interested in **inverse imaging**, particularly image/video restoration and enhancement, and **image/video quality assessment** to guide reconstruction under real-world distortions. My work combines data-efficient learning, including few-shot and contrastive methods, with uncertainty quantification and robustness under dataset shift.
